Catalogue

Twelve tools. One per Health Check area.

Every tool ships as a real spreadsheet or workbook plus a guide — not a PDF you read once and file away. Essentials is the complete, standard version, ready to run for most sites. Pro adds every version and the judgement to build your own.

Buy buttons are switched off for now — the checkout platform isn't picked yet. Everything else here, the descriptions, what's inside, who each one is for, is real. Pricing lives on the Pricing page, not here.
01Pillar

Skills Matrix & Cross-Training Planner

Essentials & Pro

A working record of who can do what, at what level, and what happens if they're off sick. Built for team leaders who currently hold that picture in their head. Answers three questions from one spreadsheet: who to put where this morning, what someone's working toward, and proof of who was signed off to do a job on the day they did it.

Inside: a 7-tab spreadsheet, a training record, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a team leader who currently carries the who-can-do-what picture in their head, and knows exactly how bad it'd be if they were off for a week.

02Foundation

Set Standards

Essentials & Pro

The discipline that decides whether a written standard actually changes anything, not another SOP template. Most sites can produce paperwork. Fewer can say whether the floor still does it that way. This gives you a register that tracks whether your standards match practice and whether anyone actually reaches for them when something goes wrong.

Inside: a standards register, an SOP template with a one-page Quick Guide version, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a site where the SOPs look fine in the folder and nobody's sure they match what actually happens on the floor.

03Foundation

KPI Blueprint Map

Essentials & Pro

Maps how your numbers connect, from the figure the board watches down to the thing an operator can see and change this shift. Most sites measure plenty. Few can say why a number moved without a week of digging. Built around one rule: mark what you can't explain rather than inventing a plausible link.

Inside: a 5-tab workbook, a printable tree canvas, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a manager who can see a number move and can't say why without a week of digging.

04Foundation

Culture

One guide, both depths

A way of handling honest mistakes and near-misses that doesn't punish the person for telling you. Most "no blame" policies quietly punish disclosure anyway. This tracks two different things separately: silence because people are afraid, and silence because nothing ever changes when they speak up.

Inside: a response tracker, a prompt card, one guide covering both depths.

Who's this forFor a site where "no blame" is the policy but people still go quiet when something's gone wrong.

05Foundation

Visual Management

Essentials & Pro

Turns raw data into something anyone can read in five seconds: are we alright right now, or not. Deliberately kept to foundation depth, so it's a starting point rather than a full workplace-organisation overhaul.

Inside: a visual controls register, a stranger-test walk sheet, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a floor where the data exists somewhere but nobody can tell at a glance whether today's alright.

06Pillar

OTIF & RFT Dashboard

Essentials & Pro

On-time-in-full and right-first-time, defined properly before they're tracked. Both terms mean something slightly different at every site that uses them, and that's usually where the argument starts. This makes you write your own definition down first, then gives you a dashboard that actually matches it.

Inside: a workbook with definitions, logs and a dashboard, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a site where "on time" means something different depending on who you ask.

07Pillar

Production Schedule Adherence Tracker

Essentials & Pro

Shows three different numbers instead of one blended figure: whether you hit volume, whether you hit it without overproducing somewhere else to cover a shortfall, and whether you hit it on time. Most trackers hide the second number, which is exactly the one that lets a bad week look fine.

Inside: a workbook with a three-number dashboard, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a manager whose hit-rate number looks fine while everyone on the floor knows a bad week happened anyway.

08Pillar

Downtime Tracking & Pareto Pack

Essentials & Pro

Tracks stoppages with a hard rule: a stop only counts as planned if it was on the schedule before the shift started. That single guard closes the most common way downtime numbers get quietly reclassified after the fact.

Inside: a workbook with definitions, a log and a dashboard, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a site that's stopped trusting its own downtime figures.

09Pillar

Waste & Cost-of-Quality Calculator

Essentials & Pro

Puts a cost against the eight lean wastes without forcing them into cost-of-quality categories they don't naturally fit. The two frameworks only overlap in one place, so this keeps them as two logs meeting at that one point, rather than mashing them together and misrepresenting both.

Inside: a workbook with two logs and a dashboard, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor someone who can feel the waste on the floor but has never had to put a number on it.

10Pillar

Shift Handover System

Essentials & Pro

A structure for the five things that need to pass between shifts, every time, so nothing depends on who happened to be on and who they happened to catch before they left.

Inside: a workbook with a handover log, an actions tracker and a dashboard, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a site where what gets passed between shifts depends entirely on who's handing over.

11Pillar

5S Audit Pack

Essentials & Pro

A scored, periodic 5S audit, built around a practitioner-authored form refined over years on real sites, not a generic checklist. Deliberately kept separate from the daily stranger-test check in Visual Management — blending the two is a documented reason 5S programmes stall. The Sustain score specifically checks whether last audit's low scores got followed up, not just whether the floor looks tidy today.

Inside: a branded audit form with live scoring, a companion workbook, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a site whose 5S looked great for the launch photo and has been sliding quietly ever since.

12Pillar

A3 Problem-Solving Templates

Essentials & Pro

A one-page problem-solving format with a built-in stop: root cause has to be verified before a single countermeasure can be written. The most freely available format in lean, so the value here is the checkpoint, not the template.

Inside: an A3 template, a register workbook tracking every A3 in flight, guides for both tiers.

Who's this forFor a team that jumps to a fix before anyone's checked it's fixing the right thing.
