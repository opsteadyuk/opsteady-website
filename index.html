How Opsteady works

Start with your site. Not a methodology.

Every manufacturing site is different. The constraint holding back one operation might be irrelevant in another. Trying to improve everything at once usually creates more activity than progress.

01

Understand where you are

Start with the Opsteady Health Check. It looks across your operation to identify where attention is most needed, what can wait, and how much change is sensible to take on.

02

Decide what to work on

Your results point you towards the areas and modules most relevant to your site. You decide what to take forward, with a clearer reason for starting there.

03

Put the module to work

Each module gives your team the knowledge, guidance and working materials needed to deploy it in your own operation, without needing a consultant alongside you.

04

Improve, sustain, then look again

Work through the improvement, give it time to settle, and rerun the Health Check periodically. As your site changes, the next priority may change with it.

A practical rhythm for improvement. Observe and understand before changing things. Stabilise what needs control. Improve from a sound baseline. Sustain what works, then keep learning.
