Common questions

What if Skills Matrix isn't built yet?
Build that first. This module depends entirely on the contingency register being current, it doesn't have a fallback estimation mode the way some other modules do. Without it, cover decisions revert to exactly the guesswork this tool exists to replace. See guide section 4.
Isn't this just extra admin during an already stressful moment?
The logging takes minutes, and it's the only reason a pattern ever becomes visible. Without it, the same expensive gap gets covered the same costly way every time, treated as bad luck rather than the structural issue it actually is. See guide section 3 and section 17.
How is this different from Labour Planning & Shift Pattern Model (P1.6)?
This module handles today's absence, decided and logged in the moment. P1.6 handles the structural, longer-horizon labour plan. When a gap here repeats within your defined window, that's the signal to take it to P1.6, not something to keep patching daily. See guide section 5, fork 1, and section 10.
What if there's genuinely nobody qualified to cover?
That's not a logging problem, it's a structural one. Escalate it straight to Labour Planning & Shift Pattern Model rather than reaching for whoever's available regardless of fit. Papering over a real gap with an unqualified option just hides the problem this module is meant to surface. See guide section 5.
Do we need to record the exact cost every time, even for small stuff?
Yes, even roughly. A decision logged without its cost becomes a record of who covered what, not something that can be trended or learned from. An estimate is fine, consistency matters more than precision here. See guide section 15.
