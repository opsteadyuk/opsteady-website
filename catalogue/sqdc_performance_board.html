Common questions

We've tried boards before and they died within weeks. Why would this one be different?
Most boards that die were showing data, not status: yesterday's number with no target next to it, and nothing said about what's being done when the two don't match. This tool is specifically the standard/actual/gap-and-response format, plus a named person updating it each shift. If your old board was missing the gap-and-response column, that's very likely why it stopped mattering to anyone before it stopped being updated. See guide section 2 and section 16.
Does the person updating the board have to be the same person filling in the gap-and-response cell?
Not necessarily, whoever owns fixing the miss can fill that part in, but decide who does what for your site before you launch rather than assuming it'll sort itself out. See guide section 4.
Do we need to launch all four categories, safety, quality, delivery, cost, at once?
No, and doing that is one of the more common ways this fails early. Get one category genuinely live first, updated every shift without chasing, before adding the second. See guide section 7.
What if we don't have agreed definitions for our metrics yet?
Get those agreed first, through KPI Definitions & Cascade Guide (F1.2). A board metric with no matching register entry is a number someone invented for the board, and it won't survive the first time someone questions it. See guide section 5.
Should we add a fifth category for people metrics, SQDCP instead of SQDC?
Only if there's a specific, currently active people metric that genuinely earns daily visibility, attendance during a turnaround, training completion during a push. Adding it by default because it feels more complete usually means it's the one category nobody actually updates. See guide section 14.
