How we price

  • 01

    Some things are free. The Health Check is one of them, because reading where you stand is common knowledge, not something we invented — charging for the label would be dishonest.

  • 02

    Prices vary by product, because the value varies. A tool that rebuilds how you measure the whole operation and a one-page template don't earn their keep the same way, and pricing them identically would be lazy, not fair.

  • 03

    Essentials is complete on its own. For most sites it's everything you need to run the tool properly — not a taster for Pro.

  • 04

    The bundle saving is permanent. There's no flash sale, no "ends Friday." What it costs today is what it costs whenever you come back.

  • 05

    If a tool doesn't earn back what it cost within a month, get in touch. That's not a slogan — email us and we'll sort it out.

If it is not right, we refund it

Ask within 30 days of purchase and we will refund you. We will not argue with you about it or ask you to prove anything.

We will ask one thing: tell us why. Not to talk you out of it. We are a small operation with no sales team, and the reason someone wanted their money back is the most useful thing anyone sends us. It is how these get better.

The same applies to bundles.
