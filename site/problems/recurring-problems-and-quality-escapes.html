Do you recognise this?

The same defects and complaints keep coming back no matter how many times you ‘fix’ them.

Why it persists

Without a real root-cause discipline, the fastest available explanation gets treated as the answer, and the fix goes in against the wrong cause.

Why the obvious response can be wrong

Retraining the operator or tightening an inspection step doesn't fix a cause you haven't actually verified. It usually just delays the same defect's return.

What needs to be established first

Whether this is a single, traceable cause or several tangled factors, and what the evidence actually says, not just what's fastest to explain.
