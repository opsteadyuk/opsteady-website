Layered Process Audit System

**The mechanism.** Pick the process steps that matter most if they drift, the ones where a missed step doesn't show up until a customer finds it. Define a real check for each. Then split checking across three organisational layers on different rhythms: the team leader or operator checking daily, the supervisor or quality lead sampling weekly, the plant or ops manager confirming monthly, not just that the process is holding, but that the daily and weekly layers are actually running. Every finding below the standard becomes a named action, not a number filed and forgotten.

Who's involved

Common questions

FAQ

How is this different from the 5S Audit Pack?

Both use a scored audit structure, but they check different things. 5S Audit Pack scores workplace organisation, Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardise, Sustain, mostly by one cross-functional group. This module scores process and quality checkpoints specifically, and its defining feature is the three-layer rotation, team leader daily, supervisor weekly, manager monthly, each layer genuinely different people. Run them as two distinct tools, even when the same site uses both. See guide section 9.

Do we need three different people, or can one person cover two layers?

Three genuinely different people. Covering two layers with one person removes the exact visibility the structure exists to provide, a supervisor sampling across shifts sees a pattern no single team leader can, and a manager confirming both layers ran sees whether the system is holding, not just whether today looks right. See guide section 14.

What do we need in place before we start?

Written process standards to audit against, from Set Standards. You cannot meaningfully score compliance to a standard that was never written down. See guide section 3 and section 17.

What happens when a checkpoint keeps scoring low no matter what we do?

Treat it as a design question before assuming it's a discipline problem. Sometimes the checkpoint itself doesn't fit how the process actually runs, and the standard needs revisiting, not the team. See guide section 16.

Is this the same as Layered Leadership Standard Work & Gemba Rhythm (P2.5)?

No, though they share the rotating-level mechanic. This module applies rotation to process and quality checkpoints specifically. P2.5 applies a similar structure to leadership routine generally. See guide section 9.
