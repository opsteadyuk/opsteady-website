OEE Management & Loss Analysis

This module teaches how to calculate and interpret Overall Equipment Effectiveness, OEE, a single measure built from three separate factors, Availability, Performance, and Quality, and how to structure those losses into a ranked view so the site can see exactly where its output is actually being lost. It builds directly on downtime data the site may already be capturing, and extends it to cover losses that a simple stop log alone never shows: running slower than it should, and producing units that don't count as good output even while the machine is technically running.

TimeCalculating a first OEE figure for one asset, once basic downtime data exists, typically takes a day or two. Capturing Performance and Quality losses properly, if they aren't already tracked, takes longer, often a couple of weeks of deliberate observation to build a reliable baseline.
Who's involvedWhoever owns the asset's downtime data already, usually maintenance or the area supervisor. Someone from quality who can supply or help define the Quality factor's data. Whoever will actually own acting on the top loss once it's ranked, planned maintenance, autonomous care, or a process improvement lead.
MaterialsA simple OEE calculation sheet and loss tree, on paper or a basic spreadsheet. Existing downtime data from Downtime Tracking & Pareto Pack (P5.1) as the starting point for Availability; no new equipment or automated data collection is required to start.
