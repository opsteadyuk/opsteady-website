Make sure actions actually get closed. Every functioning daily management system generates actions. A tier meeting surfaces one. A Gemba walk finds another. A layered process audit flags a third.

Overview

Each of those moments works exactly as intended, right up until the meeting ends, and then the action, agreed out loud by everyone in the room, goes somewhere nobody can actually see it again. Three weeks later, the same issue resurfaces, and nobody can say with confidence whether it was ever addressed, who was meant to address it, or what "addressed" was even supposed to look like.

At a glance
Time
setting up the register itself takes under an hour. The real time cost is loading the currently open actions from the last couple of weeks, which typically takes half a day the first time, since it usually means pulling scattered actions off flip charts, audit forms and memory.
Who's involved
whoever owns the register day to day, and everyone who currently generates actions through a tier meeting, Gemba walk, audit, or problem-solving routine.
What you'll need
the action register, either a physical board or a simple digital list, and access to whatever the last couple of weeks of source routines have already generated.
