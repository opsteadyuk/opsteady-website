Production Schedule Adherence Tracker

Most sites already quote a schedule number, and most of those numbers are quietly answering only one of three different questions. Attainment asks whether you made the volume you planned, and it can hit 100% while you built the wrong things. Production adherence asks whether you made the right items in the right quantities, capped so overproducing your easy line can't hide underproducing your awkward one. Timing adherence asks whether it happened when the plan said it would.

Common questions

FAQ

Why does this tool measure three separate numbers instead of one?

Because "schedule adherence" is used loosely for at least three genuinely different questions: did we make the volume we planned (attainment), did we make the right items in the right quantities (production adherence), and did it happen on time (timing adherence). A site can be strong on one and quietly failing on another, and a single blended number hides which. See guide section 1.

We already have a schedule adherence number. Do we need to start from nothing?

No. Most sites are auditing, not building from nothing. Check what your existing number is actually doing first: is it attainment dressed up as adherence, or quietly measured against whatever the schedule became after several reschedules rather than what was promised originally. See guide section 4.

What's the single most important decision when setting this up?

What you measure against: the original schedule, or the latest approved reschedule. Measuring against the original is harder to hit and more honest about what the plan actually promised. Measuring against the latest reschedule is easier to hit and easier to use to flatter the number without meaning to. See guide section 5's minimum decision fork.

Do we need to track all three numbers from day one?

No. If time is genuinely short, timing adherence on its own is a legitimate place to start, it's the simplest of the three to log and read. Add production adherence once the daily habit holds, then attainment if you want the full picture. See guide section 3.

Our attainment number looks fine. Why would we bother with the other two?

Attainment can hit 100% while you build the wrong things. A high-mix line can hit attainment easily just by running whatever's fastest, while the slower-moving SKUs fall behind. Production adherence is what actually catches that. Read all three side by side, not attainment alone. See guide section 14, failure 5.
