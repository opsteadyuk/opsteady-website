Common questions

FAQ

We already run the daily stranger-test check from Visual Management. Why do we need this too?

Because they answer different questions. The daily glance catches whether today looks right. This formal, scored audit, read across several cycles, catches whether the whole system is holding or quietly eroding over time, something no single day's glance can show. Blending the two into one tool is a documented, common reason 5S programmes stall. See guide section 1.

Why is Sustain always the lowest score?

It's the field's most consistent finding across 5S programmes, and it's a design reason, not a discipline failure. The other four S's produce a visible artefact you can point to. Sustain is the absence of visible failure, which is much harder to notice and much easier to let slip. Low once is normal. Low cycle after cycle with no action against it is the actual problem. See guide section 11 and section 14, failure 3.

Do we need a formal Visual Standards & Workplace Organisation Guide (P6.2) in place before we can audit?

Yes, in spirit. This module scores adherence to a standard, and you can't meaningfully score compliance to a standard that was never defined. If you haven't formally built P6.2, at minimum agree written expectations for what "normal" looks like in each area before your first audit, since the form's questions all score against a written expectation, not a general impression. See guide section 10.

How many questions get scored, and how?

Twenty questions, four per S, each scored 0 to 4 against a written expectation printed next to it. Most are count-based, how many unnecessary items, how many missing from their defined location, which two auditors should score the same way independently. A handful, the Shine percentage questions and the Sustain KPI-board and understanding questions, need a shared judgement call instead. See guide section 4.

How often should we audit?

Weekly for the first 90 days, then stretch to monthly and eventually quarterly once scores hold steady. Frequency should track maturity, not stay fixed at whatever pace you started with. See guide section 4 and section 14, failure 5.
