5S Audit Pack

Make workplace standards visible and sustainable. If you've built the Visual Management foundation, you already have a quick check, the stranger test, walked regularly, catching whether normal is visible at a glance. This is a different tool, not a bigger version of that one.

Overview

It's the formal, scored, periodic audit underneath it, Sort, Set in Order, Shine, Standardise, and Sustain, each scored, each tracked over time.

At a glance
Time
Half a day to agree your scale and areas, then 20 to 30 minutes per area per audit.
Who's involved
A small cross-functional group works better than one auditor alone, different eyes catch different drift.
What you'll need
The Opsteady 5S Sustain Audit Form for scoring on the floor, this workbook for logging the subtotals afterwards, and a camera or phone for photos to back up scores.
