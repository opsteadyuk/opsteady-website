Full TPM Pillars Pack

This module is explicitly an expansion pack, not a replacement for the core asset-care modules a site should already have running: Downtime Tracking (P5.1), PM Schedule Builder (P5.2), Autonomous Maintenance (P5.4), and ideally OEE Management (P5.5). Total productive maintenance, TPM, classically consists of eight pillars, and most sites only ever properly implement two or three of them, autonomous maintenance and planned maintenance among them, before either stopping there permanently or attempting all eight at once in a single ambitious programme that collapses under its own weight within months.

TimeBuilding the pillar coverage map itself typically takes half a day to a day, mostly a structured conversation reviewing what already exists. Implementing one chosen pillar as a minimal viable practice usually takes a few weeks, considerably less if it genuinely reuses an existing tool rather than building something new.
Who's involvedWhoever owns the site's OEE loss tree and existing P5 modules, since the selection of which pillar to activate should come directly from evidenced loss data, not preference.
