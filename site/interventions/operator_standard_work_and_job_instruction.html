Operator Standard Work & Job Instruction

Most critical tasks on a manufacturing floor are learned the same way: watch someone experienced do it a few times, then have a go yourself while they check in occasionally. It feels efficient, and for a while it is, but it quietly produces as many versions of "the right way" as there are experienced people teaching it. Each one has their own shortcuts, their own emphasis, their own unspoken reasons for doing a step a particular way that never gets said out loud because it's obvious to them.
