Effort

What it takes

Time
No fixed setup. Trying a first task takes 10 to 20 minutes, most of it the sanity check, not the AI step itself.
Who's involved
Whoever already owns the tedious task. Not a designated AI lead. Creating that role tends to create a bottleneck: everyone waits for the one person instead of learning the two-minute habit themselves (see section 9, and section 16 (Pro) if you have it).
Materials
Access to a general-purpose AI tool. Which one is a decision for your site, and it is a real decision, so it is worth two minutes rather than defaulting to whatever is open in a browser tab. This guide does not name a product: any recommendation would date faster than the rest of the module, and what is right here depends on what your organisation already runs and on what your data will tolerate leaving the building. The working file's prompt starters are written to work in any general-purpose AI chat tool, so none of what follows is locked to one of these.
