Common questions

FAQ

What if I can't get an honest standard time for something?

Set a first honest estimate and mark it explicitly as provisional, then revisit it once a few weeks of real data exist. A guessed standard treated as fixed from day one is worse than an honest provisional one. See the variant in section 14.

What counts as planned if a stop matches something in my register but runs long?

The planned minutes are capped at the standard, whatever runs beyond that is logged separately as overrun. A 30-minute changeover that takes 40 minutes is 30 minutes planned and 10 minutes lost, never 40 minutes planned just because it was scheduled. See section 2 and section 18, fork 2.

Do I need to build my own log if my line already has sensor or MES data?

No, extract and map that data into this same model instead of replacing it, standard events register, planned/unplanned split, sample-checking. Check specifically whether the system caps planned time at a standard or counts a scheduled event in full, the latter is the exact loophole this tool exists to close. See section 4 and section 18, fork 3.

What if I'm auditing an existing log rather than starting from nothing?

Check existing "planned" entries against both failure modes: not genuinely scheduled in advance, or an overrun that got folded in without being captured. Expect to find both, that's normal drift, not a scandal. See section 5 and section 18, fork 1.

What do I do with all the small stops that don't add up to much individually?

Set your micro-stop threshold on the Definitions tab and the workbook does the rest: any stop under it is flagged automatically and bucketed into a count and a total on the Dashboard, rather than being dropped or diluting the ranking of your named causes. Individually small, collectively significant, and dropping them hides real loss. See section 16, failure 2.
