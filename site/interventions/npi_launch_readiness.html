NPI / Launch Readiness Pack

This module gives a site a structured way to check, before a new product, process or significant change goes live, that everything it depends on is actually ready: standards written and taught, people trained and signed off, quality controls proven, capacity and rate credible, materials available, and care routines in place for any new equipment. It ends in a genuine go or no-go decision, made on evidence rather than on a date fixed months earlier for reasons that may no longer apply.

TimeA short setup to agree categories and build the view, then a fixed rhythm of brief readiness reviews from the point the launch is confirmed until the process is running stably. 10 to 15 minutes is usually enough per review once the view exists.
