Data Analysis Basics

Most sites end up holding data they don't quite know how to read. A defect count nobody's ranked. A spec sheet with limits on it and no sense of whether the process can actually hit them reliably. A cluster of scrap that might be happening in the same spot every time, or might not, nobody's checked. And, sooner or later, two numbers about the same event that don't match: production's downtime log says 40 minutes, the shift report says an hour, and there's no way to settle it except an argument.
