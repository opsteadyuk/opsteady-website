Industry 4.0 & IoT Basics

Understand where connected technology genuinely helps. 0 and IoT get treated two ways on most sites, chased as buzzwords because a competitor has them or a board wants to look modern, or dismissed as something only large, capital-rich sites can use.

Overview

Both miss the actual question, which has nothing to do with site size. It's whether connecting a specific piece of equipment would tell you something you genuinely need to know sooner than you find out today.

At a glance
Time
An hour or two per asset to run the value-check properly, considerably less than the time spent researching or pricing the sensor itself.
Who's involved
Whoever actually owns the decision the data would feed. If nobody can name that person, that's usually a sign the question itself isn't real yet.
What you'll need
Going Digital's basic discipline already in use, the same "does this actually need to be different, or does it just look modern" habit, applied here one level up, to physical connected equipment rather than paper trackers.
