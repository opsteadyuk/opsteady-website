Business Continuity Basics

**The mechanism.** List the real risks that could stop the site cold, not a generic checklist copied from somewhere else. Score each one on likelihood and impact. Write a real response for the top handful, not all of them at once. Name an owner for each response, someone who actually has to act if the risk becomes real, not whoever happened to be in the room when the register was built.
