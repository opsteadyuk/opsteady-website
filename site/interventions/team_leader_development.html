Team Leader Development Pathway

**The mechanism.** Define stages from ready to develop through to fully capable. At each stage, name the observable capabilities a candidate needs, drawn from the same four categories a team leader will eventually own in their own round, Safety, Quality, People, Delivery, and attach a real development activity to each one. Someone named signs off progression from one stage to the next, against evidence, not time served.

Common questions

FAQ

Do we need Skills Matrix, Training Plan and Supervisor Standard Work Pack fully built first?

Not fully, just in at least partial use. This pathway can be built alongside them rather than strictly after, since the capabilities it tracks are drawn from what a team leader's own round will eventually cover, but there needs to be something real underneath it before it means anything. See guide section 3 and section 17.

Why does this use the same four categories as Supervisor Standard Work Pack?

Because that's the job a candidate is actually developing toward. Tracking generic leadership qualities would leave a gap between what the pathway measures and what the role actually requires on day one. Using Safety, Quality, People and Delivery means a candidate is building toward something real, not an abstract idea of leadership. See guide section 1 and section 9.

What counts as evidence for signing off a capability?

A named development activity that was actually observed, a shadow shift, a supervised attempt, a real coaching conversation someone watched happen. Time served on its own is never enough, and the guide is explicit that sign-off has to be evidence-based or it turns into a formality. See guide section 4 and section 15.

What if someone's been waiting a long time and expects promotion on tenure?

Be direct about it: this pathway exists specifically to make the decision about demonstrated capability, not time served, and be specific about what's still genuinely missing rather than letting the conversation stay vague. See guide section 16.

What if more than one person is a genuine candidate for the same role?

Run trackers for all of them in parallel, not just the one everyone already assumed was next. Part of the pathway's value is surfacing a stronger candidate nobody had noticed yet. See guide section 13.
