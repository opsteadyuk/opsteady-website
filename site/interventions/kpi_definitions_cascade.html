Overview

It doesn't tell you what any box on it actually means, and that gap has a specific, predictable shape: the same KPI name gets calculated three different ways by three different people, and every one of them is defensible on its own terms.

At a glance
Time
Half a day for the first handful of genuinely disputed numbers. Less once the pattern's established, since most measures don't have three competing versions, only the loudest few do.
Who's involved
Everyone currently reporting a number under the disputed name, in the room together, not consulted separately. Whoever owns the measure on the Blueprint tree. Whoever holds final sign-off on the register overall, see below.
What you'll need
A built or partial KPI Blueprint tree (F1.1). Without one, start with the site's single most argued-about number instead of waiting for a tree that may not exist yet.
