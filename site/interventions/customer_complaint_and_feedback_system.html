Common questions

FAQ

What if the same issue shows up on both this log and the Non-Conformance Tracker?

Link them. If the root cause is already known and logged internally, note that the complaint confirms the external impact of something the site already knew about. If it's genuinely new, log it as its own entry and treat it as a signal the internal tracker missed. See guide section 5.

Does logging slow down responding to an upset customer?

It shouldn't, and it's worth keeping the two tasks explicitly separate: respond to the customer first, log immediately after. Logging is never meant to come before the human response. See guide section 17.

What if we don't have a formal complaint SLA in our contracts?

Then you don't need contractual stage tracking. Agree your own response timescale and hold to it, that's what Essentials covers. Formal SLAs, common in automotive and aerospace, are a Pro depth note for sites that need it. See guide section 11 and section 14.

Should "closed" mean we replied, or that the issue is actually resolved?

Actually resolved. A closure date filled in the moment a reply goes out, regardless of whether anything's actually fixed, quietly stops the metric meaning anything, and it's one of the more common ways this system fails. See guide section 3 and section 16.

What if we don't have one single channel complaints come through?

Start logging from whichever channels already exist, phone, email, a portal, rather than waiting to unify them into one system first. A complaint logged consistently from three different channels beats a single beautiful channel that isn't live yet. See guide section 4.
