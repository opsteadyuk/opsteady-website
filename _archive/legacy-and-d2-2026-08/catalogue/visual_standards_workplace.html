Common questions

How is this different from 5S Audit Pack (P6.1)?
This module defines the standard. 5S Audit Pack scores whether a station is holding to it. You can't meaningfully audit an area against a standard that was never actually set down and made visible, so this is the entry point and 5S Audit Pack sits downstream of it. See guide section 10.
We've already got Visual Management Foundations (F4) running. Why do we need this as well?
F4 applies the stranger test to a handful of critical points, safety, quality or output risks, deliberately narrow. This module applies the same underlying method, the one-point-lesson format and the four-level control ladder, comprehensively, to every station rather than just the critical few. It's the same discipline at a different scope, not a competing one. See guide section 2 and section 13.
Do we need to buy anything to start?
No. A phone camera, labels or tape, and a shadow board if you want one. Half a day per station type. Nothing more elaborate is needed to get a station to Control level. See guide section 4.
What's the four-level control ladder, in plain terms?
Indicator: a sign that tells the rule but doesn't stop the wrong thing happening. Signal: something that draws attention when things drift, an alarm or a colour change. Control: the physical layout itself shapes what a person can do, a shadow board where the wrong item visibly doesn't fit. Guarantee: the wrong thing is physically impossible. Most sites sit at Indicator without realising it. This module asks for Control or above on the stations that matter. See guide section 13.
Should we mark every station properly the first time, or make it easy to change?
Easy to change, deliberately. Use tape, labels or a shadow board before paint or engraving. A standard that's expensive to change won't get changed even when everyone agrees it should be, and it goes stale exactly like an unmaintained document. Move to something more permanent only once a standard's genuinely proven itself. See guide section 5 and section 13.
