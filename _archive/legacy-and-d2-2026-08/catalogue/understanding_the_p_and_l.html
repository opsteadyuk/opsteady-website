Common questions

I'm not a finance person. Is this really for me?
Yes, that's exactly who it's for. You're not being asked to become an accountant, only to connect numbers you already generate, waste, overtime, downtime, labour, to the one statement that decides whether those numbers get resourced and believed. See guide section 1.
Do I need exact, audited figures to start?
No. A simplified or representative P&L from finance is enough to make the walkthrough real. Exact audited figures aren't required, and if nothing's available yet, the illustrative example in section 2 lands the mechanic on its own. See guide section 3 and section 4.
Why does the worked example show a 1% shift for each line, rather than just the totals?
Because the size difference matters more than any single figure. On the worked example, a 1% shift in direct labour is worth roughly six times a 1% shift in waste, which is exactly the kind of thing that should shape where a plant manager spends limited attention. See guide section 2.
Does this replace the Waste & Cost-of-Quality Calculator, Labour Planning, or the Overtime Tracker?
No. Those trackers generate the operational numbers in the first place, scrap cost, labour hours, overtime spend. This module is where those outputs get tied back to the actual line on the P&L, not a replacement for tracking them. See guide section 9.
What if our site runs several product lines with shared overhead?
A single simplified P&L won't fit cleanly. That's a genuine finance judgement call on how overhead gets allocated, not something this guide can generalise safely, involve finance directly before treating one line's figures as the whole picture. See guide section 10.
