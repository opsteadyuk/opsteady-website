Common questions

We don't have a Blueprint tree built yet. Can we still start a register?
Yes. Start with the site's single most argued-about number instead of waiting for a tree that may not exist yet. Once a tree does exist, the register attaches to it, but the register itself doesn't depend on the tree being finished first. See guide section 4.
What if two definitions are both reasonable, and neither side is wrong?
That's usually not a data problem, it's a sign you're looking at a genuine multi-business-unit case, or two teams measuring two different things under the same name. Check which before forcing a single definition that fits nobody. See guide section 5's decision fork and section 14.
Do we need to build a register for every measure on the tree, right away?
No. Start with the numbers people already argue about. A register built by working through every box in tree order takes weeks and covers plenty of measures nobody's ever disputed. See guide section 4 and section 7.
Who actually gets to decide when two teams disagree on a definition?
Decide that before you resolve the definition itself, not after. Section 4 asks you to name who proposes a definition and who holds final sign-off for your site specifically, because the answer isn't fixed across every organisation. Skipping this step means the same argument resurfaces the next time someone wants to change the definition.
A number's been reported one way for years. Do we have to change it just because this process exists?
Only if it's genuinely disputed or genuinely wrong. If nobody's arguing about it and it's serving its purpose, register it as it stands. This tool exists for numbers where the same name means different things to different people, not to force change for its own sake.
