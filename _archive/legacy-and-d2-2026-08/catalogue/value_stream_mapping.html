What it takes

Setup timeHalf a day minimum for a first current-state map, and a further half-day to a full day for future-state design, line balancing where it applies, and building the plan. Not all in one sitting necessarily, but not stretched across weeks either; momentum matters here.
Who's involvedWhoever can walk the floor with the standing to ask questions and the patience to time things properly. Not necessarily the most senior person in the building, but someone whose presence doesn't change how the line runs while they're watching it. Future-state design and line balancing benefit from a second person, ideally someone who actually works the stations involved.
MaterialsA notebook, a stopwatch or phone timer, and the working file's templates. No software required for the first map; the point is a real, hand-drawn record of what was actually observed, not a polished diagram.
