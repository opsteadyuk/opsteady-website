The usual pattern

Most improvement effort doesn't fail from lack of trying

It fails from working on the wrong thing, in the wrong order, with no way to tell whether it stuck. Some of this will be familiar.

  • Output disappears somewhere in the middle of the shift, and nobody can point at exactly where.

  • Today's plan is out of date by lunchtime, and tomorrow's is built the same way regardless.

  • The same quality problem keeps coming back, fixed differently each time.

  • Firefighting has quietly become the actual management system.

  • Performance depends on two or three specific people being in the building.

  • Meetings produce updates, not decisions.
